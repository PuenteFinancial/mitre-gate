FAQ

Common questions.

How does an engagement start?

With a two-week assessment that ends in a ranked roadmap and a proposal. There's no long-term commitment until you've seen it.

How is Mitre paid?

Our fees are tied to the value we create and earned as we deliver, not paid all up front. We'll walk through specifics on a call.

How do you handle our data?

We work inside your systems with the access our agreement defines, and we hold to the security standards your industry expects.

Do we have to replace our current tools?

No. We connect to what you already use. Where custom software would serve you better than a tool you're paying for, we'll show you the math.

Do you work on site?

Yes, wherever we can. Our engineers embed with your team in person and work remotely when that serves the project better.

Which AI models do you use?

Whichever fit the job and your preferences. We're model-agnostic, and nothing we build locks you into one provider.
