Where AI pays off

Typical opportunities in finance.

  • Reconciliation and close

    Match transactions across ledgers, banks and subledgers, explain the breaks, and shorten the month-end close.

  • Document intake

    Extract and validate data from statements, applications, contracts and filings, and hand clean records to downstream systems.

  • Contract and policy review

    Compare agreements against your standards, highlight deviations, and give reviewers a short list instead of a stack.

  • Reporting and variance commentary

    Draft management reporting and explain what moved and why, so analysts spend their time on judgment.
